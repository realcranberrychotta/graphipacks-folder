## Users
- Primary Users: Industrial procurement managers, supply chain directors, and commercial packaging engineers across FMCG (bakery, snacks, dairy, hygiene), agribusiness (commercial growers, substrate processors), and retail food brands.
- Operating Situation: Sourcing high-volume flexible packaging, high-barrier laminate rolls, UV-stabilized agricultural films, or specialized automated packaging bags.
- Primary Job: Evaluating technical film barrier specifications and manufacturing capabilities, validating plant credibility and certifications, and requesting customized production quotes.

## Product Purpose
Graphipacks provides custom-engineered, high-performance flexible packaging solutions. The digital platform serves as the direct technical showcase and commercial conversion engine—enabling enterprise buyers to discover specialized packaging categories, inspect multi-layer film barrier structures, verify industrial credentials, and submit high-precision custom production quote requests.

## Positioning
Direct manufacturing authority backed by 40 years of continuous extrusion, printing, and conversion heritage (founded 1984; incorporated 1998 under Executive Director C.S. Arjun). Graphipacks couples in-house multi-layer extrusion (3-layer & ABA) with Rotogravure and High-Speed Flexo printing. A unique market differentiator is being a dedicated domestic manufacturer of high-precision Wicketted Poly Bags engineered for high-speed automated packing lines (Modern, Elite), alongside heavy-duty export-grade agricultural grow and coir substrate bags (Pelemix, Dutch Plantin).

## Operating Context
- Evaluation Context: High-stakes vendor qualification, annual enterprise contract tenders, and automated production-line retrofits.
- Physical Usage Environments:
  - High-speed automated food packaging lines (bread wicket dispensing and tear-off lines).
  - Harsh agricultural field exposure (UV degradation resistance and soil contact for mulch films and grow bags).
  - Heavy-duty international container shipping (puncture resistance for 50L coir pith and 5kg substrate blocks).
  - Controlled-atmosphere food and retail shelf environments (moisture, aroma, and oxygen barrier preservation).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Confirmed Capabilities:
  - Multi-layer blown film extrusion (ABA and 3-layer extruders with twist gusseting and custom UV stabilization).
  - Printing technologies: Rotogravure printing, normal flexo printing, and high-speed flexo printing.
  - Dedicated product categories: Wicketted Poly Bags, Agriculture Mulch Film, Grow Bags, Coir Pith 50L & 5kg Block Bags, Laminated Rolls & Pouches (2-layer and 3-layer barrier laminates), and PP/LLDPE Bags.
  - Technical layer breakdown: Outer BOPET (12µm, print carrier & structural strength), MET-PET barrier (7µm, moisture & gas barrier), LLDPE sealant (50µm, heat-seal closure).
  - Structured RFQ / custom quotation workflow.
